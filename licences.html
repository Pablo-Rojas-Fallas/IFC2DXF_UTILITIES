IFC2DXF web

IFC2DXF runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your computer: they are read and converted inside the browser tab, and nothing is uploaded or stored. The site makes no requests to other servers.

It is built with the open-source components below. Each one is distributed unmodified, under its own licence; the full licence texts are in the LICENSES/ folder of this site.

Coordinate reference data

Coordinate system definitions come from the EPSG Geodetic Parameter Dataset, as included in the PROJ database (proj.db) shipped with pyproj. The EPSG Dataset is owned by the International Association of Oil & Gas Producers (IOGP) and is used under the EPSG terms of use; it is not modified.
